# ERC 'Earth' — A Manifesto of Civilizational Adulthood

> *«ЖК «Земля» — Манифест взрослой цивилизации»*

A speculative manifesto written in 2037, looking back at how humanity fired its politicians, abolished borders, and finally fixed the plumbing. Includes open-source project blueprints to start building the system today — Skill-ID, AI arbitration, smart contracts, mesh networks, and more.

---

## What is this?

A book. Written as a document from the future — a resident of Earth in 2037 explaining, with mild sarcasm and engineering precision, how the old world of states, borders, and political theatre gave way to something that actually works.

It is not a political programme. It is not a utopia. It is a technical manual for a house that someone forgot to maintain for eighty years.

The book traces one chain:

**Fear united us → the enemy vanished → connections atrophied → algorithms locked people in → institutions quietly rotted → reality crashed through the wall.**

Then it describes what came next.

## Core idea in one formula

```
E = I / (P × n)
```

`E` — useful output. `I` — the idea. `P` — number of unrelated stakeholders with veto power. `n` — bureaucratic resistance.

Everything the book argues follows from this. The denominator is the problem. Always has been.
